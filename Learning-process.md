# Learning Process

## Question 1
**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

It's a way of learning where you explain a topic in very simple words, like you're teaching a kid, and whenever you get stuck you go back and study that part again.

## Question 3
**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

- **Active (focused) mode:** When you concentrate hard on one thing, like solving a problem or studying a concept. You use what you already know and think step by step.
- **Diffused mode:** When your mind is relaxed and not focused on anything, like while walking, bathing or sleeping. The brain connects ideas in the background and this helps in understanding new or difficult things.

## Question 4
**According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

1. Break the skill into small parts.
2. Learn just enough to practice and correct yourself.
3. Remove distractions that stop you from practicing.
4. Practice for at least 20 hours.

## Question 5
**What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?**

- Keep my phone on silent and turn off notifications of non-work apps while working.
- Block social media during work hours.
- Track my time with an app like Boosted to see where it is going.
- Work in focused sessions of about 25 minutes, then take a proper break like a short walk.
- Explain every concept in my own words, out loud or in writing, and go back to the parts I can't explain.
- For each concept, ask where it is used, what it is, how to code it and when I need to finish it.
- Start practicing early instead of only watching videos or reading.
- Write my own code instead of copying from tutorials.
- Use deadlines to plan my time, not to rush through topics.
- Tell my mentor early if I'm stuck on something instead of waiting.
- Stay curious and enjoy learning, and try to get really good at a few skills.
